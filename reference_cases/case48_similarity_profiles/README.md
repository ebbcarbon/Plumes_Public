# case48 — the similarity-profile selector, enumerated and measured (2026-08-25)

Run to settle **PLAN 8.4's prerequisite**: is the parabolic cross-plume profile measured over
8 617 archived rows *the* exe's behaviour, or *one setting's*? It is one setting's. The exe offers
three similarity profiles, the control is real, and each one lands on a different centreline.

Generated by `studies/pending_experiments.py` as `pending/style_enumeration`; the experiment note
with its predictions-before-the-run is kept beside this file as
[`EXPERIMENT_NOTE.md`](EXPERIMENT_NOTE.md).

## The options

The selector offers exactly three, and `Default Profile` is selected when a project opens
(operator, 2026-08-25 — nobody had seen this dialog before, which is why two earlier attempts at
this experiment were unperformable):

| GUI label | trace | as-run project |
|---|---|---|
| `Default Profile` | `style_default.dat` (2026-08-24), `style_default_rerun.dat` (2026-08-25) | `asrun_style_default.prj` |
| `3/2 Power law Profile` | `style_threehalves.dat` | — |
| `Gaussian Profile` | `style_gaussian.dat` | `asrun_style_last.prj` |

⚠️ Only two `.prj` files survive for three options: the exe rewrites the project on every run and
there is no Save-As, so `asrun_style_last.prj` is the **last** run's state, not the 3/2 one's.
The `.prj` carries no profile selection anyway (it is GUI session state, like the stop-at boxes) —
which is exactly why the selection has to be recorded here, in prose, by the person who made it.

## ⭐⭐⭐ The measurement: each option is a different profile, and two of three are identified

`Dilutn / CL-Dil` is the flux-average over the centreline — the peak-to-mean ratio of whatever
cross-plume shape the model is carrying. It is `1.0200` on the first printed row and climbs as the
plume develops; the **plateau** below is the mean over the 252 rows with `Dilutn > 5`, and it is
flat there to five figures.

| option | plateau | spread over 252 rows | profile it identifies | predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Default Profile` | **2.00000** | 1.99961 – 2.00032 | **parabolic `1 − u²`** | 2.0000 ✅ |
| `3/2 Power law Profile` | **3.88997** | 3.88881 – 3.89119 | **`[1 − u^1.5]²`** | 3.8889 ✅ |
| `Gaussian Profile` | **3.66998** | 3.66882 – 3.67129 | `exp(−k u²)`, **k ≈ 3.568** | ⛔ *not* the 2.313 predicted |

All three predictions were registered before the run. Two hold; the third is the interesting one.

### The default is the parabola, confirmed a second way

2.00000 over 252 developed rows, matching the 8 617-row measurement PLAN 6b already had. Nothing
new — but it is now known to be *the default setting's* value rather than a property of the model.

### `3/2 Power law Profile` is the reference's own profile

3.88997 against a predicted **3.8889** — the exact reciprocal of the area-average of `[1 − u^1.5]²`,
which is the profile the 3rd edition derives its `3.89` from. ⭐ **So the exe ships the manual's
profile as a non-default option**, and the long-standing contradiction PORTING_THE_PHYSICS §2
records — *"the velocity profile is parabolic, not 3/2-power"* — is not the exe disagreeing with
its own manual. It is the exe *defaulting away* from it.

⚠️ The residual is small but real: 3.88997 against 3.88889 is **+0.028 %**, about 35× the printed
precision (`CL-Dil` prints three decimals, worth ~8×10⁻⁶ relative here). Not rounding. Most likely
the exe's own quadrature over the profile; worth one look if anyone ever needs the fourth digit.

### ⛔ The Gaussian is **not** the Gaussian PLAN 6b considered, and that matters

3.66998 is nowhere near the **2.3130** of `exp(−2u²)`, the candidate PLAN 6b tabulated and the one
§8.4 assumed a move to. Reading the plateau as a truncated Gaussian `exp(−k u²)` on `u = r/b`, the
ratio is `k / (1 − e^{−k})`, so the exe's Gaussian implies

> **k ≈ 3.568** — equivalently a Gaussian cut off at **1.889** e-folding lengths.

That is a *much* more peaked profile than the literature-standard one: it puts the centreline
**3.67×** the mean where `exp(−2u²)` puts it 2.31× and the parabola 2.00×. ⚠️ **A study that
adopted "the exe's Gaussian" thinking it was adopting the jet-and-plume literature's Gaussian would
overstate its centreline by ~59 %**, and Ω_brucite's risk window is a centreline quantity.

⚠️ `k ≈ 3.568` is not a recognisable constant (it is not π, 2π, e, or 7/2), and no manual in
`upstream/` or `references/` states the exe's Gaussian truncation. So the reading above is an
*inference from one plateau*, not a decoded formula — it reproduces the number and it may not be
the parameterisation the exe actually uses.

## What this does to PLAN 8.4

8.4 has been sitting on whether to move the port from the exe-matching parabola to a Gaussian, with
the assumed cost being **leaving parity**. Two halves of that assumption are now answered and they
point in opposite directions:

- ✅ **There is no parity cost for choosing a profile.** The exe will produce a Gaussian run and a
  3/2-power run, so the port can match either.
- ⛔ **But "the exe's Gaussian" is not the literature's Gaussian.** Adopting it for physics reasons
  and adopting it for parity reasons are now clearly *different decisions*, where before they
  looked like one. The decision 8.4 has to make did not get easier; it got better specified.

## The free control: determinism across sessions

`style_default_rerun.dat` is **byte-identical** to `style_default.dat`, run a day apart in a
different exe session. `_archive_traces` de-duplicates it away, so it costs nothing in the
archive-wide sweeps and it is kept because it is evidence: the exe is deterministic across
sessions, not merely within one.

## The case

case03's geometry throughout — 25 ports, 2 m spacing, 0.0127 m ports at 2 m depth, 35 psu effluent
at 0.000219 m³/s into the case03 ambient, 0.02 m/s current, output interval 1, 410 steps.

⚠️ **This geometry never merges** (the trace prints `Plumes not merged`), so only the unmerged
column of PLAN 6b's table is exercised here. The merged values — 1.5000 parabolic, 2.2222 for
3/2-power — remain unmeasured, and a merged run under each option is the obvious follow-up.

⚠️ The discharge-to-current offset is **0°**, so `|cos| = 1` and both exe builds print the same
wastefield width (48.56 m). These traces therefore say nothing about the build split of row 275,
and cannot be miscounted by it either.
