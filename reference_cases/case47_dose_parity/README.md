# case47 — the alkalinity dose axis, and the exe's CO2SYS holds to pH 12 (2026-08-25)

**The runs Phase 9 was blocked on.** Every chemistry parity row in the ledger (35, 41, 43, 128) had
been measured at one alkalinity — TA 4000 on case03 and case04 — while the dose study sweeps to
20 000, so the study could not quote a parity-checked pH anywhere along its own axis. These five
runs put the exe's own port pH from 8.37 to **11.988** and answer the question the axis asks.

Generated by `studies/dose_parity_experiment.py`; predictions registered before the runs are in
[`EXPERIMENT_NOTE.md`](EXPERIMENT_NOTE.md).

## The runs

case03's geometry and ambient, unchanged — 25 ports, 2 m spacing, 0.0127 m ports at 2 m depth,
35 psu at 0.000219 m³/s, 0.02 m/s current, output interval 1, 410 steps each. **Only the effluent
carbonate endmember moves**, and it is entered as **TA + DIC** rather than case03's TA + pH, so the
exe's own CO2SYS computes the port pH instead of being handed it.

| trace | effluent | exe's port pH | ours | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dose_baseline_TA2930.dat` | TA 2930 / DIC 2500 | 8.370 | 8.381 | +0.011 |
| `dose_TA4000.dat` | TA 4000 / DIC 2500 | 9.148 | 9.161 | +0.013 |
| `dose_TA6000.dat` | TA 6000 / DIC 2500 | 10.628 | 10.653 | +0.025 |
| `dose_TA10000.dat` | TA 10 000 / DIC 2500 | 11.483 | 11.504 | +0.021 |
| `dose_TA20000.dat` | TA 20 000 / DIC 2500 | 11.988 | 12.008 | +0.020 |

⚠️ **The baseline is a no-dose run and it was not planned.** TA 2930 is the carbonate dialog's own
**default** (operator-confirmed) — so an unedited dialog discharges a run that looks like a dose
run and is not one. Kept because it earns its place twice over: it is the zero point of the axis,
and it is where the reader-defect below was *not* triggered.

## ⭐⭐⭐ The answer: the divergence does not run away at high pH

Feeding the exe's **own printed TA and DIC** back through PyCO2SYS on its declared constants
(K1K2 = 10 Lueker, KSO4 = 1), over all 2050 rows:

| pH band | rows | mean Δ pH (exe − ours) | mean Δ Ω_arag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.3 – 8.5 | 995 | −0.0110 | −3.10 % |
| 9.0 – 9.5 | 189 | −0.0123 | −3.06 % |
| 10.0 – 10.5 | 49 | **−0.0221** | −2.87 % |
| 10.5 – 11.0 | 53 | −0.0222 | −2.67 % |
| 11.0 – 11.5 | 126 | −0.0202 | −2.62 % |
| 11.5 – 12.0 | 58 | **−0.0196** | −2.67 % |

The registered question was whether the gap **widens above pH 11**, where borate, water and the
carbonate alkalinity terms trade places and the fitted `K1`/`K2` become minor. **It does not.** It
peaks near pH 10.5 at 0.025 and *narrows* above 11.5. Worst |Δ pH| anywhere: **0.0253**, at
pH 10.515.

⭐ So an alkalinity dose curve's pH is **parity-checked to 0.025 over its whole range**, and
Phase 9's exit criterion is met for pH with no named non-parity justification. On the strength of
these runs `chem.constants.PH_PARITY_WINDOW`'s ceiling moved **10.5 → 12.05**.

## The other predictions, all held

- **Hydrodynamics bit-identical** to case03's `kso4_option3.dat` on all 410 steps of all five runs
  — chemistry rides on top of the plume and does not feed back (row 215).
- **Ω_calcite / Ω_aragonite = 1.57990**, flat to five figures at every dose. That is row 123's
  `Ksp` ratio, unmoved by a 5× dose and 2.8 pH units, which places the whole divergence in the
  speciation rather than the solubility products.
- **TA and DIC mix conservatively**, backing out to the entered endmembers within 0.4 %.

## ⛔ Two traps these traces found, and both were ours

**1. The reader could not parse the two highest doses.** At TA 10 000 and 20 000 the plume runs
supersaturated enough that `R_cal` reaches **105 022.286 µmol/hr** — exactly the ten-column field
width — so the row prints `    37.737105022.286` with **no separator** and a whitespace split
returns one token where there are two. 191 rows do it. The `.dat` step table is fixed-width and the
*writer* had always known that; only the reader assumed whitespace. Fixed in `io/dat.py`, measured
against all 167 514 archived step rows first (zero disagreements).

**2. The chemistry columns are µmol/kg despite the `(mmol/m3)` heading.** By a dilution of 562.6
they converge to TA **2900.208** and DIC **2500.120** — the *entered* ambient values, to four
figures. Dividing by ρ = 1.02695 to "correct" the label drops every gap in the table above to
0.008 and would have quietly re-pinned rows 41/43 at the wrong value. The effluent-only ×ρ
conversion (PLAN §7.5) is visible in the same rows: the first printed DIC is 2566.1 where 2500 was
entered.

## The far-field halves (examined 2026-08-26)

All five traces carry a far-field block with chemistry (`Dilution`, `Width`, `Distance`, `Time`,
`Bckgrd`, `Conc.`, `TA`, `DIC`, `pH`, `OmegaC`, `OmegaA`). Re-solving the exe's own printed TA and
DIC through PyCO2SYS on K1K2 10 / KSO4 1, with S and T relaxing from the near-field endpoint toward
the trapping-depth ambient (2.27 m: S 31.13, T 10.59 °C) with the Brooks factor:

| trace | far-field rows | Δ pH (exe − ours), mean [min, max] | Δ Ω_arag |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dose_baseline_TA2930.dat` | 502 | −0.0113 [−0.0114, −0.0113] | −3.07 % |
| `dose_TA4000.dat` | 481 | −0.0108 [−0.0114, −0.0104] | −3.07 % |
| `dose_TA6000.dat` | 481 | −0.0108 [−0.0114, −0.0104] | −3.07 % |
| `dose_TA10000.dat` | 481 | −0.0107 [−0.0114, −0.0104] | −3.07 % |
| `dose_TA20000.dat` | 481 | −0.0108 [−0.0114, −0.0104] | −3.07 % |

Three things, none surprising and all worth having written down:

- **The gap is the ambient-end gap** (rows 36/41: 0.008–0.011), flat along the far field because
  by the transition the plume *is* the ambient end — the first far-field pH is 8.372–8.410 whatever
  the dose. The dose axis's 0.025 peak lives in the near field, above pH 10.
- **The seam is exact.** The first far-field row repeats the near field's last on TA, pH and
  dilution (baseline: TA 2900.21, pH 8.372, dilution 562.63 on both sides), which is the
  construction `results._farfield_chemistry` uses — the far field's first chemistry row equals the
  near field's last by construction. Every trace asymptotes to **TA 2900.0 / DIC 2500.0**, the
  entered ambient at the trapping depth (row 56), in µmol/kg — the same unit finding as trap 2.
- ⚠️ **The four dose runs stopped on a 5 000× dilution stop, not the declared 10 000×.** All four
  end at 481.662 m / 5 026.7 (bit-identical far fields, since the hydrodynamics are); the
  baseline, run in a different session, ran to 502.608 m at 5 295.6 with the distance binding.
  `experiments.check_farfield_session_state(dat, case)` reports it on all four (*"matches neither
  declared rule"*) — the session-state drift it exists to catch, caught. It moves nothing in the
  near field and nothing in the table above; it does move row 277's census to **7** traces on a
  5 000× stop (102 / 12 / 5 / 7 / 2 over 128 tables).

`dose_parity.prj` carries `nearfield_flags[3]` = **3**, the rise/fall count the note asked for.

⚠️ The discharge-to-current offset is 0°, so `|cos| = 1` and both exe builds print the same
wastefield width (48.56 m). These traces cannot be classified by the row 275 split, and cannot be
miscounted by it either.
